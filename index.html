I'm sorry

For a silence that lasted far too long.

scroll ↓

A year ago, I blocked you.

You had done nothing to deserve it. I was overwhelmed and afraid of falling behind, and I let that pressure turn me into someone who pushed away a friend without a word.

I've felt the weight of that many times since. I can't take it back. I wish I could.

It was my mistake. No pressure could justify it, and I'm truly sorry for the hurt I caused, and for the quiet I left behind.

I've missed having you in my life.

If you can ever find it in your heart to forgive me, I would be grateful.

And if you can't, I understand. You owe me nothing. I only wanted you to know that I'm sorry.

— Aman
